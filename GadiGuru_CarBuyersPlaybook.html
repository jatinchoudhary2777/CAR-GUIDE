Step 01 — Fuel Type
WHAT'S YOUR
POISON?

Petrol, Diesel, CNG, or Electric? Each has a vibe. Pick what actually works for your life, not what your relatives say.

⛽
Petrol
The safe bet. Always has been.
  • Lower upfront cost
  • Smoother driving, less vibration
  • Easy to maintain anywhere
  • Petrol pumps literally everywhere
  • Pricier per km than diesel/CNG
  • Not ideal above 1500 km/month
Best for: City driving, under 1200 km/month, first car buyers, anyone who doesn't wanna overthink it.
🛢️
Diesel
Highway king. Numbers don't lie.
  • Best mileage for long drives
  • Torquey performance (esp. SUVs)
  • Lower fuel cost per km
  • Higher purchase price (₹1-2L gap)
  • Noisy at idle (some models)
  • Maintenance costlier
  • Not viable in NCR/some cities
Best for: Highway heavy-users, 2000+ km/month, SUV lovers. Break-even at ~60,000 km from petrol variant.
💨
CNG
Budget savior. Truly no flex.
  • Cheapest per km (₹1.5–2/km)
  • Great for high daily usage
  • Eco-friendly alternative
  • Many factory-fitted options now
  • Boot space eaten by CNG kit
  • CNG pumps still limited
  • Slightly lower power output
  • Queue at pumps (in some cities)
Best for: Ola/Uber drivers, daily 80-100 km commuters, metro city residents with nearby CNG pumps.
⚡
Electric
The future is now, kinda.
  • ₹0.8–1.5/km running cost
  • Instant torque, fun to drive
  • No emissions, tax benefits
  • Charging infrastructure growing fast
  • Range anxiety still real
  • Long highway charging waits
  • High upfront price
  • Apartment charging still a pain
Best for: Bungalow/villa owners, short-range city commuters, tech-forward buyers, 2nd car in a household.

The Real India Stuff
THINGS THAT
ACTUALLY MATTER

Stuff no YouTube reviewer tells you because they're too busy talking about 0-100 timings on a government road.

🛣️
Ground Clearance
Minimum 165mm for city driving, 180mm+ for anything remotely non-urban. The pothole map of India is not the same as Germany. Never buy anything under 160mm without a dedicated parking spot and smooth colony roads.
🌡️
AC Performance
India is not a temperate country. Check rear AC vents (not just a token vent, actual airflow), cooling capacity in 45°C heat, and sunroof insulation. Budget cars often have underpowered ACs — test drive in afternoon heat, not in the showroom's 22°C AC.
🔧
Service Network
Maruti's 4500+ service centers are not an accident. Check if the brand has authorized service centers within 20 km of your home AND your parents' home. A great car stuck at an unfamiliar service center on a highway is a nightmare you don't want.
📋
On-Road vs Ex-Showroom
The gap is typically 12–18%. Includes: registration charges (varies by state), insurance (1st year mandatory), road tax, accessories, handling charges. Always ask for an on-road price quote — that's your real number. Never plan around ex-showroom price.
🏙️
NCR Diesel Rules
Delhi-NCR has banned diesel vehicles over 10 years old. If you're buying diesel in NCR, factor this in — especially for used cars. BS4 diesel vehicles also face restrictions. Check your city's specific norms before going diesel.
📱
Connected Car Features
Features like remote AC start, real-time tracking, OTA updates are actually useful here. Check if the connected car subscription is free for life or expires in 3 years (many do). Tata's iRA and Hyundai's BlueLink are currently best-in-class.
Step 05 — Used Car Guide
RED FLAGS
INCOMING 🚩

If you see any of these during a used car inspection, run. These are the exact things dealers hope you miss.

🎨
Paint Mismatch
Shine a flashlight at panel joints. Different shades between panels = repainted after an accident. Check every panel individually — door edges, roof corners, under the bonnet.
Get it inspected by a panel beater, not a friend
🔢
Odometer Tampering
Wear on steering wheel, gear knob, and floor mats vs the odometer reading should match. A "20,000 km" car with a worn-out seat bolster is a red flag. Check service records for odometer history.
Get CARFAX/Vahaan verification done
📄
RC & NOC Issues
Make sure the RC matches the chassis number physically embossed on the car. If the car is from another state, get a No Objection Certificate (NOC) before buying — non-negotiable.
Check RC on Parivahan portal (parivahan.gov.in)
💧
Flood-Damaged Cars
Lift floor mats and check for watermarks or rust underneath. Check ECU and fuse box for water residue. Smell the AC vents for musty odour. Flood cars look fine outside but rot from inside.
Avoid cars from flood-prone cities after monsoon
🔩
Misaligned Panels
Stand behind the car and look at the gap between all body panels. Uneven gaps mean the car was in an accident and repaired. Even a "minor" repair means the structural geometry is altered.
Crouch down and eyeball all panel gaps
🔋
Electrical Gremlins
Check every single button — windows, mirrors, sunroof, AC blower speeds. Check dashboard for warning lights that someone might have taped over. Start the car cold to check smoke colour.
Blue smoke = oil burn. Black = rich fuel. Both = nope
🏦
Loan Hypothecation
If there's a bank name on the RC (hypothecation entry), the original owner may still have a loan. You could lose the car to the bank even after paying the seller. Always get NOC from the financier.
Hypothecation removal is mandatory before purchase
📊
Overpriced "Inspected" Tags
OLX Autos, Cars24, Spinny charge premiums for "certified" inspections. Their inspection is basic — get an independent third-party inspection done for ₹1,000-2,000. Money very well spent.
CarDekho or Spinny inspections ≠ mechanic inspection

Step 06 — Insurance
INSURANCE
DECODED

Every term your dealer throws at you, explained without the jargon. Because getting ripped on insurance is the oldest trick in the book.

IDV
Insured Declared Value — the current market value of your car. This is the max you'll get if your car is stolen or totalled. Higher IDV = higher premium but better coverage.
💡 Don't let dealers lower IDV to reduce premium. You'll regret it at claim time.
NCB
No Claim Bonus — discount for not making a claim. Goes from 20% in Year 1 to 50% after 5 claim-free years. Most valuable asset in your insurance history.
💡 Never make small claims. Lose NCB over a ₹5,000 scratch = lose ₹30,000+ over time.
Third-Party
Legally mandatory in India. Covers damage to the other person/property but NOT your own car. Cheap but basically the bare minimum. Never enough by itself.
💡 Mandatory by law. New cars get 3-year TP bundled. Never go without it.
Comprehensive
Third-party + your own car damage (own damage/OD cover). This is what you actually need. Covers theft, natural disasters, accidents. Always get this.
💡 Go comprehensive for any car under 10 years old. Full stop.
Depreciation
By default, insurance pays you IDV minus depreciation on parts when you claim. That plastic bumper is depreciated at 50% after just 2 years. Ouch.
💡 Zero-Dep add-on removes this pain. Worth it for the first 3 years minimum.
Deductible
The amount you must pay before insurance kicks in. Compulsory deductible is fixed by IRDAI. Voluntary deductible reduces your premium but increases your out-of-pocket costs at claim time.
💡 Don't take high voluntary deductibles just to save ₹500 annual premium.
Zero Dep ✅
No depreciation deducted at claim. Absolutely worth it for new cars in the first 3 years. Cost: ₹2,000-5,000/year but saves massive at claims. Non-negotiable for new cars.
RSA ✅
Roadside Assistance. Towing, flat tyre help, fuel delivery, battery jump-start. Especially useful in India where breakdowns in unfamiliar locations are genuinely stressful. Get it.
Engine Protect ✅
Covers hydrostatic lock (engine damage from waterlogged roads). In monsoon India, this is NOT optional. One claim pays for 10 years of add-on cost. Buy it if you live in flood-prone cities.
NCB Protect ✅
Allows one claim per year without losing your NCB. If you've built 40-50% NCB, this is precious. Losing NCB over a small claim is heartbreaking.
Tyre Protect ⚠️
Covers tyre damage from road hazards. Maybe useful in extremely pothole-prone areas. Otherwise skip — tyre replacement rarely exceeds ₹6,000 and you don't want to waste a claim on it.
Consumables ⚠️
Covers nuts, bolts, lubricants at repair time. Cheap add-on but rarely makes a significant difference. Nice to have if the cost is minimal, but not a priority.
Buy Online
Never buy insurance from the dealer at delivery. You'll pay 20-40% more. Go to PolicyBazaar, Acko, or ICICI Lombard directly. Same coverage, way lower cost. This is the biggest single saving most new car buyers miss.
NCR Transfer
If you're moving cities, transfer the NCB immediately with your new policy. The NCB certificate is yours personally, not tied to the car. Don't abandon 50% NCB when switching policies!
Claim Timing
Don't claim for anything under ₹15,000-20,000 in the first 5 years. Protecting your NCB is worth more long-term. Calculate the math before raising a claim — it often doesn't make financial sense.
Garage Network
Choose insurers with cashless garages near your home. ICICI Lombard and Bajaj Allianz have the widest networks. At claim time, cashless is dramatically less stressful than reimbursement.
Bonus — Glossary
EVERY TERM
EXPLAINED

From ABS to TPMS, no more nodding along at the showroom without knowing what they're saying.

ABS
Anti-lock Braking System. Prevents wheels from locking during hard braking so you can still steer. Mandatory on all new cars in India since 2019.
ADAS
Advanced Driver Assistance Systems. Includes lane-keep assist, collision warning, adaptive cruise control. Level 2 means it steers AND brakes automatically in limited scenarios.
Torque
Rotational force. Think of it as pulling power — how well the car accelerates from low speeds. High torque at low RPM = better for city traffic and highway overtaking.
BHP
Brake Horse Power. Top-end speed potential. More BHP = higher top speed potential. But for India, torque matters more than BHP in real-world driving.
EBD
Electronic Brakeforce Distribution. Works with ABS to distribute braking force between front and rear wheels based on load. Makes braking more stable under heavy loads.
ESP
Electronic Stability Program. Detects and prevents skidding by selectively braking individual wheels. Critical safety feature — check if it's standard, not an add-on.
Hill Assist
Prevents the car from rolling back on a slope when you release the brake. Super useful in hilly cities like Shimla, Pune, Shillong. Often called HAC (Hill Assist Control).
CVT
Continuously Variable Transmission. Smooth but can feel rubbery. Great for city traffic, less engaging on highways. Fuel efficient when driven gently.
DCT
Dual-Clutch Transmission. Two clutches — one for odd gears, one for even. Lightning fast shifts. Used in performance-oriented automatics (Kia, Volkswagen, Hyundai). Can be jerky at very low speeds.
AMT
Automated Manual Transmission. Cheap automat option popular in budget cars. Smooth at highway speeds, slightly jerky in stop-go traffic. Best way to get AT without spending much.
BS6
Bharat Stage 6 emission standard. Equivalent to Euro 6. All cars sold in India since April 2020 must comply. BS6 Phase 2 with OBD-II is now mandatory from 2023.
Ground Clearance
Distance between the lowest point of the car and the road. Higher = handles bumps, speed breakers, and flooding better. Critical spec for Indian roads. Check unladen clearance.
ARAI Mileage
Lab test result under ideal conditions. Real-world mileage is typically 15-25% lower. Always mentally reduce ARAI claims. City driving is always worse than highway.
Ex-Showroom
Base price without registration, insurance, and taxes. Never the price you'll actually pay. Add 12-18% to get a rough on-road estimate depending on your state.
MG (G-NCAP)
Global New Car Assessment Programme (India). Independent safety rating from 0-5 stars. Most meaningful metric for safety — ignore brand claims and look up actual G-NCAP scores at safernewcars.com
OBD Port
On-Board Diagnostics port (under dashboard). Mechanics plug in here to read error codes. You can buy a cheap Bluetooth OBD reader to read codes yourself with a free app.
TPMS
Tyre Pressure Monitoring System. Alerts when any tyre is under-inflated. Mandatory on all new cars from April 2022. Saves tyres, improves fuel efficiency.
OTA Updates
Over-The-Air software updates. Your car's software gets updated like your phone — wirelessly. Feature additions, bug fixes, improved performance. Tata, MG, Kia do this well.
